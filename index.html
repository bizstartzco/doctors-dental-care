Why Doctors Dental Care

Many treatments, one clinic, the right doctor.

Every treatment plan starts with understanding what brought you in. You are then seen by the doctor on our panel whose work suits your treatment, with tests done in our own lab when you need them.

  • Full range under one roof

    Preventive, restorative, surgical and orthodontic care in one clinic, so you are not sent across town for common procedures.

  • A panel of doctors, not one

    Each patient is matched with the doctor whose experience fits the treatment. You can see who they are and choose when you book.

  • Our own laboratory

    Sukra House of Diagnostic does blood tests and other investigations on site, so results reach your doctor quickly.

Your visit

What happens when you get in touch

Four simple steps, and you always know what comes next.

  1. 01Book your time

    Choose a treatment, a doctor and a free slot online, or call or WhatsApp us.

  2. 02Examination

    Your doctor looks at the problem, orders X-rays or lab tests if needed and explains what they find.

  3. 03Your options

    We go through the treatment options and what each involves, so you can decide.

  4. 04Treatment and follow-up

    Treatment is carried out, with clear aftercare advice and a review where needed.

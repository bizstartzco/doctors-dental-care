Why Doctors Dental Care

One clinic for the whole of your dental care.

Every treatment plan starts with understanding what brought you in, whether that is routine maintenance or a more involved restorative need.

  • Full range in one place

    From a simple filling to complete dentures and implant work, so you are not passed between clinics for common procedures.

  • Early mornings and evenings, every day

    Open 7:30 AM to 7:30 PM, seven days a week, so you can come before work, after school or on Saturday.

  • Seen by the dentist

    Your care is led by Dr. Navin Sah, consultant dental surgeon.

Your visit

What happens when you get in touch

  1. Get in touch

    Call, WhatsApp or send the request form. We will confirm a time that suits you.

  2. Examination

    We look at the problem, take X-rays if needed and explain what we find in plain language.

  3. Your options

    We go through the treatment options and what each involves, so you can decide.

  4. Treatment and follow-up

    Treatment is carried out, with clear aftercare advice and a review where needed.

Privacy notice

This page explains what happens to the information you give us through this website.

The appointment form

The form asks for your name and phone number, and optionally your email, a preferred date, the treatment you are interested in and a message. We use these details only to contact you about your appointment. We do not sell them or use them for advertising.

WhatsApp and phone

WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp, which is run by Meta and covered by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy. Phone links simply start a call.

Map

The map is provided by Google Maps and only loads if you press “Show map here” or open Google Maps. Google then receives information such as your IP address, under Google’s privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Your health information

Please do not send detailed medical records through the form. We will ask for what we need at your visit.

Asking us to delete your details

Call us on +977 981846600 or message us on WhatsApp and we will delete the details you sent through the website.

Crowns & bridges

A crown is a cap that covers the whole visible part of a tooth to protect and restore it. A bridge, also called a fixed partial denture, replaces a missing tooth by anchoring a false tooth to crowns on the teeth either side.

A crown or bridge may help when

  • A back tooth has had root canal treatment
  • A tooth is cracked, heavily filled or badly worn
  • A large filling keeps breaking
  • A tooth is discoloured or misshapen and a filling will not do
  • You have a gap from a missing tooth with healthy teeth on either side

Zirconia or metal-ceramic?

Metal-ceramic

Porcelain fused over a metal base. Strong and long-established. Over time a thin dark line can sometimes show at the gum.

Zirconia

Metal-free and tooth-coloured all the way through, and very strong. A popular choice where appearance matters.

We will recommend a material based on where the tooth is, how you bite, and your budget.

What happens, step by step

  1. Preparing the tooth

    Under local anaesthetic the tooth is shaped so the crown can fit over it.

  2. Impression and shade

    We take an impression and match the shade to your other teeth.

  3. Temporary crown

    A temporary crown protects the tooth while the permanent one is made.

  4. Fitting

    At the next visit the permanent crown or bridge is checked for fit and bite, then cemented in place.

How many visits? Usually two visits.

Afterwards

  • Your bite may feel different for a day or two. If it still feels high, come back for a quick adjustment.
  • Brush and floss as normal. Under a bridge, use a floss threader or interdental brush to clean the gap.
  • Avoid using your teeth to open packets or bite very hard objects.

Questions about crowns & bridges

Does preparing a tooth for a crown hurt?

It is done under local anaesthetic. The tooth can be a little sensitive for a few days afterwards.

How do I clean under a bridge?

Food collects under the false tooth, so thread floss or a small interdental brush under it every day. We will show you how.

Can a crown be whitened later?

No. Whitening does not change the colour of crowns, so if you want whiter teeth, whiten first and match the crown afterwards.

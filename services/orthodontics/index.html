Treatment

Orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment moves teeth into better positions, using gentle, steady pressure over time. It can straighten crowded teeth, close gaps and improve how the teeth bite together.

Orthodontics may help with

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Front teeth that stick out
  • A bite where upper and lower teeth do not meet properly
  • Teeth that are hard to clean because of their position

Small gaps between front teeth can sometimes be closed without braces, with tooth-coloured bonding. We will tell you which approach suits you.

What happens, step by step

  1. Assessment

    We examine your teeth, bite and jaw and discuss what you want to change.

  2. Records

    Photos, X-rays and impressions are taken to plan treatment.

  3. Fitting

    The appliance is fitted and we explain how to look after it.

  4. Adjustments

    Regular appointments to adjust the appliance and check progress.

  5. Retention

    Once the teeth are in place, retainers hold them there.

How many visits? Treatment usually runs for months to a couple of years, with regular adjustment visits.

Afterwards

  • Teeth often feel sore for a few days after fitting or an adjustment.
  • Cleaning takes extra care. We will show you how to brush and clean around the appliance.
  • Avoid very hard or sticky food that can break braces.
  • Wear your retainer exactly as advised. Without it, teeth tend to drift back.

Questions about orthodontics

Am I too old for braces?

No. Adults can have orthodontic treatment as long as the teeth and gums are healthy.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on how much the teeth need to move. Many cases take from several months to two years. Your plan will give an estimate.

Why do I need a retainer?

Teeth naturally try to move back towards their old positions. A retainer keeps the result.

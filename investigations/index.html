Lab & tests

Blood tests and X-rays in our own lab

Our own diagnostic laboratory works alongside the clinic, so blood tests and other investigations can be done in the same visit, without a separate trip across town. We do all kinds of routine and specialised tests. If you do not see a test below, ask us. We most likely do it.

In-house laboratory

Sukra House of Diagnostic

Tests are done on site and reported straight to your doctor. Your doctor will tell you which tests you need, and you can also book a test your doctor has already asked for.

Haematology

  • Complete blood count (CBC)

Biochemistry

Preparation and booking

Blood test

Bleeding time / clotting time (BT/CT)

Checks how well blood clots, sometimes requested before extractions or surgery.

Preparation
Tell us about any blood-thinning medicines.
Results
Sample: e.g. same day
Book this test

Complete blood count (CBC)

A routine blood test that may be requested before oral surgery.

Preparation
No fasting needed unless told otherwise.
Results
Sample: e.g. same day
Book this test

Fasting blood sugar

Checks blood sugar, important before surgery and implants for people with or at risk of diabetes.

Preparation
Fast for 8–10 hours before the test (water is fine).
Results
Sample: e.g. same day
Book this test

HbA1c

Shows average blood sugar over about three months.

Preparation
No fasting needed.
Results
Sample: e.g. same day
Book this test

Dental X-ray

Intraoral dental X-ray (IOPA)

A small X-ray of one or two teeth and their roots, used to check decay, infection and root canals.

Preparation
No preparation needed. Tell us if you are or may be pregnant.
Results
Sample: e.g. same day
Book this test

Panoramic X-ray (OPG)

A single X-ray of all the teeth and both jaws, used for wisdom teeth, implants and orthodontic planning.

Preparation
Remove earrings, necklaces and hairpins. Tell us if you are or may be pregnant.
Results
Sample: e.g. same day
Book this test

Before a test: some blood tests need fasting, so ask us when you book. Tell us before any X-ray if you are or might be pregnant, and bring a list of your medicines.
